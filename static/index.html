Chrome or Firefox recommended. Mic and music are mixed together in your browser before sending, so they're always in sync in the outgoing stream -- there's nothing to keep in sync server-side. The Monitor tap is local audio routing (not a round trip), so it stays essentially instant even while listeners are hearing you a second or more behind.

On connect, this measures round-trip time to the server 3x and snaps to the smallest shared buffer tier your connection can reliably hit. Everyone who lands on the same tier hears identical content at the identical instant, computed independently -- no coordination between listeners needed. A very slow connection will land on the largest tier and get flagged "degraded" rather than dragging everyone else's delay up.
